VelocePDF

Fast, minimal MuPDF reader inspired by SumatraPDF
Sumatra-inspired

SumatraPDF proves a reader can be under 10 MB, launch instantly, and stay out of your way. VelocePDF follows that model with a single window, tabs, Frequently Read, Command Palette, and MuPDF for accurate, fast rendering in a restrained Tabler layout.

Instant
Visible-page rendering, bounded prefetch, and worker-isolated parsing keep the UI responsive.
Private
JS and launch actions are disabled, links need confirmation, and no network is used while reading.
